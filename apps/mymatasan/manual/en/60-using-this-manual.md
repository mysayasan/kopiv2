# Using and printing this manual

This manual is part of the appliance, not a website it links out to. It works with no internet
connection, it is the version that matches the software you are actually running, and it can be
printed.

## Finding things {#finding}

The rail on the left lists every article, grouped by section. The **search box** above it searches
the full text of every article, not just the titles — so searching for a phrase you half-remember
from a screen will usually find the page that explains it.

Articles link to each other. Following a link keeps you inside the manual.

## Contextual help {#contextual}

The **?** buttons scattered through the product open this manual in a panel beside whatever you
were doing, already scrolled to the relevant part. Close the panel and you are back where you
were, with nothing lost.

The manual is also available before you sign in, from the link on the sign-in screen. That is
deliberate: the questions people have while staring at a sign-in screen are the ones they cannot
sign in to answer.

## Languages {#languages}

The manual follows the language you picked for the product. If an article has not been translated
into that language yet, it is shown in English rather than left blank, and labelled as such.

## Printing {#printing}

Two buttons sit at the top of every article:

- **Print this page** prints the open article.
- **Print the whole manual** prints everything, in order, with a table of contents at the front.

Both open your browser's normal print dialogue, so **Save as PDF** is available wherever your
browser offers it. That route is worth preferring over any other, because it renders every
language correctly — including Chinese and Arabic — and gives you a file you can keep with the
site's other documentation.

The printed version drops the navigation, the search box and the buttons, and keeps tables,
warnings and code blocks from being split across a page break.

> [!NOTE]
> A printed manual goes out of date. Print it when you commission a site or after a significant
> upgrade, and check the version stamped in the printed header before trusting a page that
> disagrees with what is in front of you.
