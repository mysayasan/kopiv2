# Frequently asked questions

## Does it need the internet? {#internet}

No. Detection, recording, search, alerts and this manual all run inside the appliance.

Three things reach out if you use them, and all are optional: downloading a stock model variant
the first time you select it, the in-app ffmpeg and AI-runtime installers, and any notification
destination you configure (Telegram, a remote webhook).

## Does my footage go anywhere? {#privacy}

No. Recordings stay on this machine's storage and are encrypted there. Nothing uploads them.

The exceptions are what you set up yourself: a notification destination configured to include
snapshots sends those images wherever you pointed it, and a clip you download goes wherever you
put it.

## How many cameras can it handle? {#how-many}

It depends on the hardware, the detection stream resolution and how many models are active — which
is why the product measures rather than guesses. Run **Settings → Machine Health → Run
calibration**. See [Storage and capacity](storage-and-capacity#estimate).

## How much disk do I need? {#disk}

`cameras × bitrate × retention days`, roughly. Decide retention from how long it actually takes
somebody at your site to review an incident, then size from that. See
[Storage and capacity](storage-and-capacity#retention-policy).

## Why is there no alert when there is clearly footage of something? {#no-alert}

Recording and detection are separate. Recording writes footage; **rules** produce alerts. A camera
with recording on and no rules records faithfully and tells you nothing. See
[Creating detection rules](detection-rules).

## Can I use it with cameras that are not ONVIF? {#non-onvif}

Yes. ONVIF makes discovery and management convenient, but a camera that speaks RTSP can be added
by address. See [Adding by address](adding-cameras#by-address).

## Why can my colleague not open recordings? {#viewer}

They are a **viewer**. Viewers watch live video and see that alerts happened; reviewing recorded
footage is the operator line. See [Users and roles](users-and-roles#roles).

## Why can nobody delete footage except an administrator? {#delete}

Deliberately. An operator who was present at an incident cannot destroy the footage of it — that is
what makes the recording trustworthy afterwards. See
[why operator cannot delete](users-and-roles#evidentiary).

## Can I get alerts on my phone? {#phone}

Yes — Telegram is the quickest route with no infrastructure of your own; a webhook or MQTT works if
you already have somewhere to send them. See
[Notification destinations](notification-destinations).

## Can I run it on a Raspberry Pi? {#raspberry-pi}

Yes, within reason. Stay on the **nano** or **small** stock model, point detection at sub-streams,
and expect a handful of cameras rather than dozens. Calibrate before you commit to a number.

## Do I need a GPU? {#gpu}

No, but it changes what is practical. On CPU, stay small and few. A GPU makes larger models and
more cameras realistic, and turns model *training* from hours into minutes.

## What happens if the machine dies? {#machine-dies}

With a [configuration backup](backup-and-restore) and an exported
[recovery key](encryption-at-rest#export), you rebuild on new hardware and the copied recordings
decrypt.

Without the recovery key, the recordings are unreadable **permanently**. Export it today.

## Does an update lose anything? {#update-loss}

No, but it restarts the appliance — so live view drops and recording pauses for the restart. Take
a backup first anyway. See [Updates, restart and health](updates-and-restart#updating).

## Can two people use it at once? {#concurrent}

Yes. Each person signs in with their own account, and layout, language and theme are remembered
per browser. Give people their own accounts rather than sharing one — a shared login destroys the
value of every other control here.

## Why does the camera preview look choppy while I draw a zone? {#choppy-preview}

The preview is a periodic snapshot, not live video, and it pauses while you drag. Detection runs
at full rate on the real stream regardless — the stuttering preview says nothing about detector
performance. See [the preview](camera-properties#preview).

## Can I recognise faces? {#faces}

Yes, and there are legal obligations attached. Faceprints are biometric data and in many
jurisdictions require the person's consent before enrolment. See
[People and face recognition](people#consent).

## Is fire detection a fire alarm? {#fire-alarm}

No. It is a camera noticing something that looks like fire, and it does not replace smoke
detectors, sprinklers or any life-safety system you are required to have. See
[Fire and smoke](fire-smoke-and-plates#fire).

## Where do I find the version? {#version}

The page footer, and **Settings → Version & Health**. Quote the app version, core version and
commit when reporting anything.
